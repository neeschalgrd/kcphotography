About

Hi, I'm KC.

I photograph weddings and events for people who would rather be present than pose. Most of my day is spent quietly in the background, watching for the glance across the room or the laugh during the speeches.

I love low light: reception candles, golden hour, a dance floor lit by one string of bulbs. My editing keeps skin tones true and colours warm, so the photos still feel like the day decades from now.

Based in Australia and happy to travel. Every gallery is delivered online, ready to download and share.
