About

Hi, I'm Ayush.

I'm Ayush KC, the KC behind KC Photography. I photograph the milestones families want to remember: maternity shoots, weaning ceremonies, birthdays, weddings and events.

Most of my time is spent quietly in the background, watching for the small moments: a hand resting on the bump, a grandparent's blessing, a toddler meeting their first cake. When it's time for portraits, I guide gently so everyone feels relaxed.

My editing keeps skin tones true and colours warm, so the photos still feel like the day years from now. Based in Australia and happy to travel. Every gallery is delivered online, ready to download and share.
